## 6 · Anything else — my read

**The assumption everything above rests on.** Your four games carry four
different developer credits: Blend In Or Die, Boxing Club! (Offical), Red Lab
Studios, Dog Poundd. Studios that build their own catalogue publish under one
group. Four credits reads as a portfolio — acquired, published, or operated. So
I wrote you as an operator, which makes "We buy Roblox games" the thesis of the
site rather than a footnote. **If you built all four yourself under separate
groups, tell me and I'll flip the voice back to a builder's.**

**The stat tile is honest but lopsided.** Paint And SEEK is 104.6M of the
105.8M total — 99%. The number isn't wrong, but anyone who opens the catalogue
sees where it comes from. Two options if that bothers you: lead the stats with
concurrent players (590 across four games reads as a healthier spread), or say
nothing and let it stand. I'd let it stand; big numbers are the point.

**Cut the build log unless you'll feed it.** It's five rows of dated updates and
I can fill two, one of which hasn't happened yet. A stale build log is worse
than no build log — it advertises the last time you shipped. Either send me a
real history or let me drop the block and let the studio values fill that column.

**The acquisitions section is a promise, not decoration.** Right now it says
you'll make a written offer in 72 hours and pay a multiple of monthly revenue.
If you aren't ready to do that for whoever emails tomorrow, soften it before it
goes live — the process steps can stay while the numbers come out.

    Game acquisitions

    We buy Roblox games.

    If you built something with real players and ran out of time, energy or Robux to keep it running, we'll take it over. Your name stays on the game page, we take on the updates, the moderation and the hosting, and you get paid.

    Start a conversation

    120k CCU The owners have owned games at that scale before this studio. We have run the servers, the economy and the moderation at peak, not just read about it.

    What we look for

    We look for high quality games built by ambitious people. While retention plays an important role in our offers, it will not make or break the deal.

    1. We reach out to YOU

      One of our contractors or scouts will contact you and ask if you're interested in exploring a deal with us.

    2. We review your numbers

      We review your game's stats, retention, and revenue to pitch a fair price to you.

    3. We finalize the deal

      We finalize the deal, and successfully purchase the revenue share or experience from you.
